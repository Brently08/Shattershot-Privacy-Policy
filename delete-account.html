Shattershot lets you play as a guest or create a player account with a username and password. Your account, ticket balance, settings and progress are stored only on your own device. We do not keep a copy on any server, so there is nothing stored with us to delete. You can remove everything yourself using either method below.

Option 1: Uninstall the game

  1. Press and hold the Shattershot icon on your device.
  2. Choose Uninstall (Android) or Remove App → Delete App (iPhone/iPad).

This permanently removes your account, password, tickets, stats and settings from your device.

Option 2: Clear the game's data (keep the app installed)

Android: Settings → Apps → Shattershot → Storage → Clear data.

iPhone / iPad: deleting and reinstalling the app is the way to clear its data.

After clearing, the game starts fresh and you can play as a guest or create a new account.

What is removed

  • Your username and (scrambled) password
  • Your ticket balance, daily bonus streak and personal bests
  • Your sound, haptics and other settings

Deleted data cannot be recovered, because no backup exists.

Ads

If you chose to watch optional rewarded ads, ad data is handled by the ad provider under its own privacy policy. You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your phone's settings.
