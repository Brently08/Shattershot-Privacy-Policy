Privacy Policy

App: Shattershot  |  Developer: Brain Freeze Studios  |  Last updated: October 18, 2026

Brain Freeze Studios ("we", "us", or "our") built Shattershot as a free-to-play mobile game. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Shattershot does not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information. All game progress, settings, and ticket balances are stored locally on your device only using Godot's built-in save file system (user://). This data never leaves your device.

Advertising

Shattershot includes an optional "Watch Ad" feature that allows players to earn in-game tickets by viewing a rewarded video advertisement. When this feature is used, a third-party ad SDK may collect certain non-personal, anonymized device information (such as device type and ad interaction data) in accordance with that provider's own privacy policy. We do not receive or store any personal information from this process. You are never required to watch an ad to play the game.

In-App Purchases

Shattershot may offer optional in-app purchases through Apple App Store or Google Play. All transactions are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We do not collect, store, or have access to any payment information.

Children's Privacy

Shattershot is a general-audience shooting gallery game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data from any user, no special handling is required. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Device Permissions

Shattershot may request access to device vibration (haptics) for gameplay feedback. No other device permissions are required. We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other sensitive data.

Data Retention

All locally stored data (game saves, settings) remains on your device and can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services

Shattershot does not integrate any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or social login services at this time. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
